# Elder Care Coordination Platform

A comprehensive family eldercare management system coordinating care among family members, medical providers, and caregivers through shared care plans, medication tracking, and caregiver marketplace integration.

## Overview

Caring for elderly family members involves coordination across multiple family members, healthcare providers, and professional caregivers. This platform centralizes communication, scheduling, and documentation to ensure consistent, high-quality care while reducing stress on family caregivers.

## Core Features

### Comprehensive Care Plans
- Medical needs and treatment protocols
- Daily living assistance requirements
- Dietary restrictions and meal planning
- Therapy and rehabilitation schedules
- Medication regimens with dosing details
- Emergency response procedures

### Medication Management
- Complete medication lists with dosages
- Automated refill tracking and alerts
- Drug interaction warnings
- Medication schedule reminders
- Adherence monitoring and reporting
- Pharmacy integration

### Appointment Coordination
- Multi-specialist appointment scheduling
- Transportation arrangement coordination
- Family member attendance coordination
- Medical record sharing
- Appointment reminder system
- Post-visit notes and follow-ups

### Family Communication Hub
- Shared updates and photo sharing
- Task assignment and tracking
- Care responsibility distribution
- Emergency contact coordination
- Document sharing (legal, medical, insurance)
- Decision-making collaboration

### Document Management
- Wills and estate planning documents
- Powers of attorney (medical and financial)
- Advance directives and living wills
- Insurance policies and claims
- Medical records and test results
- Emergency information cards

### Caregiver Marketplace
- Vetted professional caregiver network
- Skill-based matching (medical, companion, specialized)
- Availability and scheduling
- Background check verification
- Reviews and ratings system
- Direct communication platform

### Care Team Coordination
- Role definition (family, paid caregivers, medical)
- Shift scheduling and handoff protocols
- Care notes and status updates
- Quality metrics and monitoring
- Communication channels
- Training resources

## Smart Contracts

### care-organizer.clar
Manages care plans, schedules, and family coordination:
- Care plan creation and updates
- Medication tracking with interaction checking
- Appointment scheduling across providers
- Family task assignment and tracking
- Document storage and access control
- Emergency contact management
- Care transition tracking (home, hospital, facility)

### caregiver-network.clar
Connects families with professional caregivers:
- Caregiver registration and verification
- Skill and certification tracking
- Availability and scheduling management
- Payment and time tracking
- Quality monitoring through feedback
- Care team coordination
- Training resource provision

## Getting Started

### Family Members
1. Create elderly individual profile
2. Input medical conditions and medications
3. Invite family members to care circle
4. Assign tasks and responsibilities
5. Upload important documents
6. Connect with professional caregivers as needed

### Professional Caregivers
1. Register with credentials and background check
2. Complete platform training
3. Set availability and service areas
4. Accept care assignments
5. Document care provided
6. Build reputation through quality work

### Healthcare Providers
1. Register as medical provider
2. Access patient care plans
3. Update medication and treatment plans
4. Coordinate with care team
5. Monitor compliance and outcomes
